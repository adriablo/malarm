New in 0.6.1:
- Full-screen alarm: larger Snooze/Custom/Dismiss buttons, centered on screen
- New-alarm dialog: label first, -15 to +15 minute step buttons, Save/Cancel at the bottom, sensible default time
- Top app bar with app name; Settings moved into its menu
- Exports named malarm-alarms with timestamp; F-Droid link in Settings
- Reliability: dismissing an alarm cancels its pending snooze; cancelling the snooze picker stops the ringing
- Alarms recover after a force-stop and re-anchor when the clock or timezone changes
- Back button on the ringing screen snoozes instead of leaving it ringing; snooze timing survives manual clock changes
